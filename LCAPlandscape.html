Overview

    What the LCAP does not show. A tall bar here is not evidence of science professional learning, and a short one is not evidence of its absence. District science PL is commonly funded outside the LCAP: site plans (SPSA) and site Title I set-asides, federal Title II-A and Title IV-A, the Educator Effectiveness and Learning Recovery block grants, CTE Incentive Grant and Perkins (which pay for pathway lines like Poway's), general fund, and grants such as Coronado's DoDEA award. Staffing is the better signal: Poway's $3.3M STEAM line sits in a district whose Learning Support Services page lists no science or STEAM TOSA or coordinator (its CTE office is an executive director, a director and five Career Education Specialists), while Carlsbad's 1.0 FTE K-12 Science Specialist, confirmed in the October 17 audit, appears in no LCAP action.     Why it matters for LCAPs

    With science now a full Dashboard indicator, a district whose student group lands in the lowest performance level on science can expect that result to count toward state and federal support determinations, which is the trigger for Differentiated Assistance and for required LCAP focus goals. Most district plans reviewed here were written before the October 15, 2026 Dashboard release, so the 2027-28 LCAP cycle (spring 2027 drafts, June adoption) is the first in which science results will shape goals and actions.

    Sources and method

    Method

    Every district's adopted 2026-27 LCAP was downloaded as a PDF (San Diego County from the SDCOE School Finder district directory; Imperial County from the CDE LCAP service by CDS code) and converted to text with page numbers preserved. Heber Elementary's PDF carried no usable text layer and was processed with OCR after rotation. Every row of every Goals and Actions table was parsed (action number, title, description, Total Funds, Contributing) and kept when the description contained science, NGSS, STEM, STEAM, Next Generation Science or CAST. A second pass searched for climate, environmental and engineering. Matches that were only "science of reading", "social science", "computer science", "improvement science", "science of learning", "school climate", a school name, or the CAST (Center for Applied Special Technology) acronym were removed by hand. Metrics, plan summaries, annual updates and Priority 2 self-ratings were read separately. Every quote and dollar figure was checked against the page cited.

    Categories. A = science professional learning (PD, coaching, TOSAs, release time, PLCs). B = science curriculum or instructional materials. C = student-facing science program, staffing, enrichment, field work, or CTE/STEM pathway. Science-specific means the action is about science (or STEM/STEAM) and its Total Funds can reasonably be attributed to science; named within broader action means science is one component of a multi-subject or general action, and the Total Funds shown is the whole action.

    Limitations

    • LCAP figures are planned 2026-27 budgets, not actual expenditures; where the annual update reports an estimated actual for 2025-26 it is quoted in the action notes.
    • Bundled actions overstate science spending; the science-specific totals understate it, because districts fund science from site budgets, grants and general fund outside the LCAP (Del Mar's STEAM+ program and Coronado's DoDEA STEM grant are examples).
    • Table text occasionally interleaves the Title and Description columns; titles were reconstructed from context and every quote was re-read on its page.
    • CAST figures are reported as each plan states them; percent met or exceeded and Dashboard science points are different scales.
    • The staffing roster combines the October 17, 2026 audit with an October 19 web pass; 23 entities could not be re-verified because their sites block automated reading.
